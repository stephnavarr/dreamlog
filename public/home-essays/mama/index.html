Mama

it'll be okay

Mama, I want to ask you about you.

But I’m scared of what you’ll say. I’m scared that the stories you’ll tell will make my sadness stay.

I feel tired, Mama. How do I ask you for help?

When today I am the one holding you, telling you it’ll be okay.
