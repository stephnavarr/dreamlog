to be be pink and then be blue
and to burn and to be born
again and again.

to be brave and to be broken
to be home and to be displaced
again and again.

to stand and to fall and to love,
to have my soul crushed,
and my wings ripped out, 
to stand in the mirror
and watch my back bleed,
again and again.

my kismet
to live and to die 
again and again,
give my soul 
to these strangers
again and again.

why?

oh,
to love is to be alive.

oh,
to love is to fly
to run away,
to flee and to find myself
again and again and again.
