---
title: "Excuse Me, Do You Know That Place?"
date: 2022-11-04
excerpt: "Can you take me there?"
---

Excuse me, 
do you know that place?

Where the table is set,
Forks and knives lined neatly 
next to clean plates
topped with warm meals?

Have you seen it?

Have you felt it?

Sinking into a hug after a long day,
a long week, 
a long time.

Can you take me there?

I can’t count on myself to find it anymore.

Can you take me?

So I can lay my head and rest,
free my mind of the burdens I've carried,
the burdens I've made.

Lay me down.
Tell me the world is still mine.

Tell me, 
the world is still mine.
