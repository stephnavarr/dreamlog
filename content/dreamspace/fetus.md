---
title: "the fetus"
date: 2026-08-11
excerpt: "this is about something ancient, intimate, and vulnerable that takes over the body and a sense of self."
---

The fetus
Unfurls its body
From the small black stone it wrapped itself around.

It stretches,
Reaching its arms back, 
back back,
sending its fingers branching up through my shoulders
my neck
my skull.

Its legs go down,
down down,
toes creeping into my stomach
my legs
my thighs
all the way down to my ankles.

I feel my body grow leaden
My blood
turned to stone.

A gray cast covers my eyes,
the world around me
drifts away.

My mouth is frozen shut
lips too heavy to scream.

I can't be mad at it
It's just a baby
An ancient thing
born and reborn inside of me.

Gone and back again
Gone and back.
