---
title: "scream"
date: 20224-07-05
excerpt: "crying is less destructive"
---
I bow my head to the forest,
forgive me in advance.

I'm draped by the trees
as the wave rolls through me.

I lift my face up
I can't see the sky.

I can't contain her anymore.

I open my mouth
and scream.

The sound 
crashes out of me.

Water
pours from my eyes
Wind
escapes from my ears.

Fire ripples all around me
trees uprooted
mountains split open
creatures spill from their recesses
making tracks
far away from here.

And it goes on
and on
until I am empty
until everything is bare.

I feel the cry of the birds
circling
their former home.

I'm on my knees 
amid surrendered trees.

I just cry and cry and cry and cry and cry.
